# AI Trading Operating System

## Mission
Reduce manual investment research and decision-making effort while improving research coverage, consistency, evidence quality, risk discipline, decision traceability, and evaluation.

*Note: This system does NOT promise guaranteed returns or "100x returns". The measurable objective is dramatically faster and more systematic research and decision support.*

## Primary Workflows
1. **Discover opportunity:** Continuously scan the market for setups matching specific technical, fundamental, or thematic criteria.
2. **Research security:** Gather comprehensive historical and real-time data across price, volume, news, filings, and macroeconomic indicators.
3. **Multi-agent analysis:** Delegate research to independent, specialized AI agents (e.g., Technical, Fundamental, Sentiment) to process data concurrently.
4. **Adversarial debate:** Pit a Bull Agent against a Bear Agent to construct and deconstruct the thesis, mediated by a Risk Agent.
5. **Investment Committee:** Synthesize the debate into an auditable final decision (PASS, WATCH, CONDITIONAL, APPROVE) based on structured evidence.
6. **Risk validation:** Run deterministic Python logic to calculate exact position sizing, stop distance, and portfolio exposure limits.
7. **Trade plan:** Generate a concrete, actionable plan with precise entry, exit, and invalidation parameters.
8. **Paper trade:** Execute the trade plan in a simulated environment to forward-test assumptions without capital risk.
9. **Monitor:** Continuously track open positions against their original thesis and invalidation conditions.
10. **Evaluate:** Run periodic performance learning loops to assess agent accuracy, strategy edge, and system drift.
